** Overview of main idea, and Partial film overview
*** Focus initially on Bamboo Cutter & CPK
One of the first times that realization hit me was during my viewing of the films "The Tale of Princess Kaguya" (2013) and "Cosmic Princess Kaguya" (2026). As one might assume by their names, both films are retellings of the traditional Japanese story The Tale of the Bamboo Cutter. Both films were beautiful, with the 2026 film's more futuristic take on the story in direct conversation with the more traditional story in the 2013 film. Despite how different the two films were, music was immensely important in both, with Kaguya being born with a song in her heart in the first film, and the main character being a songwriter in the second. One thing that struck me was how Cosmic Princess Kaguya played with musical styles in its soundtrack, combining traditional Japanese instruments with more modern electronic sounds, further emphasizing music's impact across time.
